# Courses: where a class is, and when

Classes, instructors and rooms on the map, from the registrar's course API (`wapi.unh.edu/dhub`, what courses.unh.edu reads) and 25Live's academic calendar. Nothing here is enrolment: a class is a place and a time to get to, like an event. See [storage.md](storage.md) for what's kept and [reminders.md](reminders.md) for class reminders.

## The nouns

The sources keep these apart, and so does `src/courses/course.mts`:

- A **course** is the catalog's "CS 518". Its page lists what it needs and unlocks ([programs.md](programs.md)), the programs naming it, and its sections each term.
- A **section** is a term's offering: a CRN, a title, instructors and meeting patterns. Its page leads with the next meeting.
- A **meeting pattern** is days, a time, dates and a room; CS 518 meets in N343 Tuesday and Thursday and N113 Friday, so a section is never one event at one place. A pattern is a line on the section's page.
- A **dated meeting** is a pattern on one day, the only thing with a start, end and place. It projects to the event shape (`CourseEvent`), so it lists, routes and reminds like one. Its id is keyed by what the meeting is (days, start, room, first date), so a refetch that reorders a section changes nothing saved.
- An **instructor** (`people.mts`) is keyed by the hidden address where the registrar gives one, else the name. Their page says scheduled teaching, never whereabouts or office hours: the next class across every section they're on in a live term, and the week. It links out to RateMyProfessors' search of UNH for their name (`rmpUrl`), as their line on a section's page does; nothing of it is read or shown, as it has no API and forbids scraping.
- A **room** (`rooms.mts`) is the registrar's text, "Kingsbury N343", indexed with its building. Its page is today's schedule there; it says a room is free only where 25Live books it too ([Rooms to book](#rooms-to-book)).

`standing(term, section, calendar, now)` gives the meeting under way and the next, whatever order the source lists meetings in. A meeting is a session ([recurrence.md](recurrence.md)): its weekdays as the academic calendar has them meet, between its dates. No-class days, swap days (a Tuesday run as a Wednesday) and holidays come from 25Live's all-day entries by their titles' pattern (`calendar.mts`), which `scripts/map/courses.mts` prints for a person to check once a term. The same entries give `calendar.dates`, the dates worth knowing ahead for Durham's undergraduates: deadlines, registration, breaks and the terms' own (`KeyDate`), in the registrar's words less its capitals, a registration window by who it's for first ("Seniors · registration opens", "Spring 2027 · 8:30am"); Law's, online, graduate and staff dates are left out.

## Terms

Terms are a set, never "the" term. A term runs from its first class through its finals, where the calendar gives them (`termSpan`). The pipeline publishes every term running or starting within 150 days that has sections; pages load the **live** ones, running or starting within two weeks (`activeTerms`), so in late January J-Term and Spring, and the terms to come only for a course's page. Saved sections are keyed `term:CRN` (`202610:12496`) and read against the live terms, so a past term drops out by itself.

## The files

`scripts/map/courses.mts` writes the index, `src/courses/courses.json` (the calendar, and each term's dates, freshness and file), and each term's sections under `public/data/courses/`, named by content. The index loads with the map; a term file loads once something asks for courses (typing in search, a page, a saved section, a class reminder), and the service worker keeps it ([offline.md](offline.md)). A term's `fetched` is the registrar's timestamp on its oldest page; `built` is when the file was written.

The import fails loud (`registrar.mts`): an unreadable row names its position and field, and a term whose rows don't add up to the API's total or that lists a CRN twice is refused. Codes for nowhere (online, TBA, off campus) keep their text and place nothing. A building code `buildings.mts` can't place, a new one or one whose building the gazetteer no longer names, is never guessed at: its classes keep their text and place nothing too, so the night's other inputs still publish, and the Inputs run then fails, naming the code, until it's added.

Instructors' addresses are hidden at build time (`email.mts`), shifted and reversed so no address regex matches the shipped JSON or HTML, and the build refuses anything that looks like one. A `mailto:` is assembled only as Email is tapped. UNH publishes the same addresses; this only keeps them from scrapers of static files.

## On the map

- **Search** (`search.mts`), in the home's search and Directions' To: a code in any spelling, a CRN or a pasted schedule, a room with or without its building, titles fuzzily, and people's names, in labelled groups. An instructor's row already says their next class, time, room and Directions.
- **Pages** (`src/map/ui/Pages.tsx`, `src/map/state/page.mts`): `/map/section/202610/12496`, `person/<id>`, `room/Kingsbury+N343`, `course/CS+518`. Directions and Remind are for the meeting the page leads with; Remind on a section goes on meeting after meeting, with nothing saved. A place's page lists its rooms with a class under way, never a count of people.
- **Save** (`saved.mts`, `courses-saved`, synced per person): your next class is a row of Yours on the home, and My courses in You (`/map/you`) has the week, saved sections, your calendar feed, and one field to add more: a code or title lists the live terms' matching sections by course as it's typed, a few courses then the rest on asking, each saved or forgotten with its star, and CRNs or a pasted schedule save in one go. Cross-listed sections meet together, so either saved is the one class. Classes never make markers or rows in the week.
- **Calendar files** (`ics.mts`, [recurrence.md](recurrence.md#calendars-out)): a section's page adds or subscribes to its meetings, and your saved sections are a feed, with their [finals](#finals) once you're verified. Each pattern is one VEVENT with a weekly rule and EXDATEs for days it won't meet, and a VEVENT more for a day swapped to its weekday, as its session says.
- **Programs**: a course's page names the programs listing it ("In 6 programs"), each opening its graph focused on the course, `/map/program/<slug>?course=<code>`; `/map/program` lists them ([programs.md](programs.md)).

## Finals

The registrar publishes each term's Final Exam Schedule about mid-semester, a spreadsheet linked from its examinations page (`unh.edu/registrar/registration-resources/examinations`) as an anonymous SharePoint share, "TBA" till then. `scripts/map/finals.mts` reads the page each night for the links, which change every term, and each linked sheet of a term the index lists (`src/courses/finals.mts`).

- **Fetching**: the share answers 403 until the cookies its redirect sets are sent back, so the download (`&download=1`) follows its redirects by hand, keeping them for the host that set them, as a browser would; one request at a time, saying `notunh/1 (+https://notunh.app)`. Nothing signs in: a redirect to a sign-in fails, as does anything but a zip at the end. It's the inputs' most fragile step.
- **Reading**: an .xlsx is a zip of XML, read with the zip the timetable's read with (fflate): the first sheet, its shared strings, each cell where its reference puts it. Columns are found by their headers (CRN, Exam Date, Times, Bldg, Room). A row is a CRN in a room at a time; a common exam has a row per room and never says who sits where, so its rooms are "one of"; a few sections sit in parts, on two days, so a CRN has a sitting per time its rows name. A row that can't be read fails the sheet, naming the row and what of it, never what it says, as the run's log is public. The sheet lists every campus, and only the finals of the term's own sections, Durham's, are kept; a sheet with none of them fails, as does a page that no longer says "Final Exam Schedule". Rooms are placed by the registrar's building code as meetings are (`buildings.mts`), and written as the term's own meetings write that building ("Paul College Business&Econ 165"), so a final's room opens the room's page; ONLINE and OFFCMP are nowhere. A code the table lacks is kept as written, and fails the run.
- **Who's told**: the sheet names no one, but finals are said only to a device whose person holds a verified UNH address, as booking titles are ([Rooms to book](#rooms-to-book)). So they're never an input, whose releases anyone can download, nor in the build: the script runs with learning, once the tests pass, and stores them whole in the Worker beside the models (`finals`, by the day read), merged over what's stored, so a term a night doesn't read keeps its finals till a week after them. A sheet that fails stores nothing, and the Worker keeps the last good finals; what's stored is checked against the shape the Worker reads first. A Worker deployed before it stored finals is only warned of. `GET /api/finals/:term` answers a verified device with every section's in the term, and anyone else 403 ([worker.md](worker.md#api)). Anyone sees the term's finals window, which the academic calendar gives.
- **On a device** (`src/map/state/finals.mts`): once the Worker says this device is verified (`/api/me`, in You), it asks for each live term's finals and keeps them on this device alone (`finals`), so they show offline; the Worker saying the device isn't verified, or refusing it, forgets them, and a term no longer live is dropped. The term stays live through its finals, so they're there in finals week. A section's page has its final, each sitting, with its room, or "one of" a common exam's, each opening the room's page, and once its meetings are over it leads with the final, with Directions where it's in one building. A cross-listed section without a row of its own takes its twin's. Your saved sections' finals are listed in My courses, a clash with another flagged, and are in your week; the next few days' join Yours, and the first further out can be Plan ahead's row of yours. A device the Worker says isn't verified is told, on a section's page while the term's finals are still to come, to verify in You for its time and room.
- **Calendar**: your feed has your saved sections' finals, each sitting a VEVENT (`course-<term>-<crn>-final@notunh.app`, then `-final-2`), while you hold a verified address; a section's own file, which anyone may fetch, doesn't.

## Rooms to book

UNH books nearly every space through 25Live Pro (`25live.collegenet.com/pro/unh`, a NetID to book): Dimond's and Kingsbury's study rooms, the MUB, classrooms. Its REST data is readable without signing in and has no CORS, so the build and the Worker read it (`src/courses/bookings.mts`), never the browser.

- **Spaces**: `spaces.json`, one request. Kept are rooms in a Durham building the gazetteer places (`placeMatcher`), not info tables, offices, lounges or lactation rooms; Manchester and the law school are left out. A space is its id, building, room ("221_A", said "221A"), what 25Live calls it ("Collaboration Space"), its capacity where it's more than 25Live's placeholder one, and whether it's a study room. Its page is `/map/room/Dimond+Library+121`; the registrar's text for the same room, `Kingsbury N343`, opens it too (`spaceFor` in `spaces.mts`), so a room is one page whoever names it.
- **Bookings**: `rm_reservations.json` for a day, of some spaces or all, as `{ space, start, end, type, title?, org? }`. Classes are booked "(Private)" and left out, as the registrar's sections say them; cancelled ones too. An exam taken with Student Accessibility Services' accommodations ("SAS" with an accommodation, exam or test, "Student Accessibility", or an accommodated exam, in its name, type or organization) is dropped as it's read (`isAccommodation`), before the Worker caches or answers anything, verified or not. Who booked (`last_mod_user`, a NetID) is never read, nor is `event.json`, which names a requester.
- **Who's told a title**: any title may name a person ("Ava and Flossie study anatomy"), so titles are said only to a device whose person holds a verified UNH address ([social.md](social.md#identity)), decided by the Worker per request from the device's secret or cookie, without making it a device (`isVerifiedAsker`). Anyone else is told the kind and the organization, unless that's none or students as themselves ("Durham - Student"): "Study group", "Meeting · Theta Chi". The build's snapshot, which anyone can fetch, has no titles (`shownTo`).
- **Fresh as it's needed**: the build bundles the spaces and the coming week (`src/map/rooms.json`, `scripts/map/rooms.mts`), so a room's page opens at once, offline too. `GET /api/rooms/:day` is 25Live's word on a day of the build's rooms, edge-cached ten minutes (25Live takes seconds over it); `GET /api/rooms/:day/:space` one room's, cached 30 s, asked as its page opens, a day is picked, or **Reserve in 25Live** is tapped, which opens the room's own page there. Only yesterday through a month ahead, and only the rooms the served build bundles, are asked of 25Live; its failing is cached 30 s. Answers merge space by space by when each was fetched (`learned`), so a day's answer cached longer never undoes a room's asked since, whatever order they arrive in, and the page changes in place.
- **Free**: what's busy in a room is its bookings and, where the registrar schedules classes there, its classes. Its page leads with "Free till 3:00 PM" or "Busy till 2:00 PM", then the day's bookings and classes together, any day of the week. A room counts as free when nothing's on for 30 minutes, or to the day's end.
- **Campus Rec** (`rec.mts`): Hamel's courts, studios and rooms, the fields and pools, from Campus Rec's Innosoft Fusion site (`crec.unh.edu`), no sign-in. Its facilities are the `<option>`s of its schedule page, placed by the last part of their path ("Bremner Field", a place of its own) else the whole; a court is within its gym, which booked books the court too (`within`, `bookingsOf`). Each facility's week is one request at build time, which is all the map knows of them. Fusion answers DevExtreme appointments: a first occurrence on the local clock with no offset, an RFC 5545 rule (DAILY, WEEKLY with BYDAY and INTERVAL, MONTHLY with BYSETPOS, COUNT, UNTIL in UTC) and exceptions, also local; its window doesn't trim recurring appointments, whose first occurrences come back from years before, so each is expanded, then kept to the week (`occurrencesOf`). What's on there ("Badminton Club") is public and is the booking's kind.
- **A place's page** lists its rooms to book, free ones first, four at first and the rest on a tap; a field that's a space of its own is one "Today's schedule" row.
- **Search** (`createSpaceSearch`), the home search's own group before the courses': a building by name, alias or first word ("MUB", "Dimond"), a room's number or name ("Strafford"), a size ("6 people", "for 6", "6+"), a study room, and a time ("free now", "free at 3pm", "open at 3:30 tomorrow"); free rooms first, study rooms first, then the smallest big enough. A building's name alone finds the building, not its rooms.

## Settled

- **HSSC**, the registrar's "Health Services Science Center", is the Health Sciences Simulation Center at 25 Colovos Rd, between Gregg Hall and Ritzman Lab. OSM draws it unnamed (way 1342295228), so `src/places/added.mts` names it with the extract's outline and door until a generation does.
- **Section restrictions** (majors, class, campus) aren't shipped: `RESTRICTIONS` is HTML prose with its sense embedded ("Only listed…", "…not allowed"), on 37 % of sections. A section's page links to `courses.unh.edu/class/<term>/<crn>`, which gives seats and restrictions as the registrar does.
