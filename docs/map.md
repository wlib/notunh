# Map

The map's sheet, and what it takes from OpenStreetMap. Buses are in [transit.md](transit.md).

## The sheet

- **Home** (`src/map/ui/Home.tsx`): search (`ui/Search.tsx`), You and Directions, then one page in two parts, **Today** and **Campus**, each named in the display type in a bar that stays at the top as the page scrolls; a name is a link to its part (`#today`, `#campus`) that scrolls the sheet's body there without touching the address or the history, and the one in view is lit. **Today** is one day read down: what you're due at next, or at now and till when, as the one card; then **Dining** while a hall is serving or opens within the hour (`diningNow` in `src/dining/now.mts`), each hall's row with its standing and the meal's highlights going straight to `/dining/?hall=<id>`, and nothing once they're closed for the night; Now; then the next four things today with what's yours today (saved classes, the next of your calendars, saved sessions, followed activities' next sessions, events you're going to) in its place among them by its time, washed in gold (the card's own once, on the card, unless it's an event, which stays so a star never moves under the tap), what recurs folded by kind, and "+ New event". Then **Yours** beyond today (with deadlines and breaks in the next few days, reminders and saved trips) and **Plan ahead** in three rows with **This week ›** and **Full term ›** ([social.md](social.md#saves-follows-and-yours)). With nothing of yours it's the day's list alone. **Campus** (`ui/Campus.tsx`) is a directory in one order, each row saying what's so now in place of a description: Dining (the meal on, and till when the halls are open, or when the next opens, from the same `diningNow`; neither the menus nor UNH Dining's pages give meal times, only hall hours), Laundry (your starred hall's machines free, else how many halls have a washer), Buses, Clubs & activities, Events this week, Classes, Parking (how many areas fit your permit for the next two hours, or with none how many are free and how many paid, as the parking page ranks them), Term dates. Typed searches lay suggestions over the page, still mounted.
- **Dining, laundry and classes in the sheet** (`ui/Campus.tsx`, `state/campus.mts`): `/map/dining` is the two halls side by side, each with its standing, the walk and what the meal leads with (`highlights` in `src/dining/menus.mts`: mains before sides, the stations that change before the everyday ones, never a counter like a salad bar or a sauce, each named as a person says it); `/map/laundry` every hall's washers and dryers free, starred first (`laundry-favorites`, the laundry page's own); `/map/classes` your week and the ways into the courses. While dining's or laundry's is shown the map says the same where the buildings stand, as a symbol layer that keeps its marks apart (`render/status.mts`), and frames the halls, or the residence halls around the middle of campus; off those pages it says nothing of either. A hall's place leads with its meal and a residence hall's with its machines, each linking on to `/dining/?hall=<id>`, which shows that hall for the visit without changing the one remembered, or `/laundry/<room>/`, whose **‹ Map** is Back itself when the visit came from the map (`PageHead` in `src/shell/ui.tsx`, `src/shell/index.mts`). It's a page's one way back: a laundry room opened from the list has **‹ All rooms** in its place. Today's menu files and `/api/laundry/rooms` are asked for only once something shows them.
- **Pages** (`link.mts`, `state/page.mts`, `ui/Pages.tsx`): every other view is a shareable path in history, `/map/<kind>/<what>`, spaces as `+`, its scroll kept for Back. Besides those in [courses.md](courses.md), [programs.md](programs.md), [parking.md](parking.md) and [social.md](social.md): `place/<name>`, `stop/<id>`, `bus/<vehicle>`, `buses`, `directions`, `new[/<place>]`, `explore`, `activity/<key>`, `week`, `ahead` and `you` (`#link=`, `#verify=` open it). `?to=<place>`, `laundry/<room>` or `dining/<hall>` plans a trip and opens Directions.
- **Detents** (`ui/sheet.mts`, `detents.mts`): lowered, half, tall, each a fixed height, any page. The sheet alone holds which; the rest asks it to `raise()` for something new in it or `expand()` for a planning view. Its grabber and header stay put; only the body under them scrolls. Dragging and scrolling are one motion: below tall, a pull anywhere moves the sheet, and a pull up that reaches tall runs on into scrolling the body; at tall, a pull up with more to show is the browser's scroll as ever, and a pull down takes the body back to its top and then lowers the sheet, in the one pull. A touch's first move decides whose it is (`owner`), and `placed` says where a drag's travel has the sheet and the scroll. Let go, `landing` says where: a flick goes on one detent that way, never two, and a slower release rests at the nearest; a finger held still before it lifts doesn't flick; let go in the scroll, that carries on by itself and slows. A touch becomes a drag only once it moves, and never from a field, so a tap leaves the sheet's layout alone. A page in the sheet **swipes back**: a touch whose first move is to the right is the page's (`owner`, where `canSwipeBack`: a page shown, not within 20 px of the screen's left edge, which is Safari's own swipe back in a tab, and not on a row that scrolls sideways or a field), which follows the finger by translate and, let go a third of the way across or flicked (`swipesBack`), slides off and goes Back, to the page it was opened over or, from the first page the visit opened, home; else it springs back. The home has nothing to go back to. It's lifted above iOS's keyboard, which iOS draws over the page: its floor is the visual viewport's bottom measured from the app, never from `innerHeight`, which iOS shrinks with it while it scrolls to a field. The page, which never scrolls, is scrolled straight back when iOS scrolls it to show a field, as that leaves taps landing away from what's drawn.
- **Camera**: `followPage` (`render/map.mts`) alone drives it, easing once as a page opens and again only when its target changes (`aimed`, `render/camera.mts`); Directions' target is the whole trip. Views mark their place with `state.focusAt(page, spot)`, written only while that page is shown. bruh runs a detached node's effects till it's collected: keep side effects out of reactive JSX.
- **Taps** (`render/taps.mts`, `index.tsx`): a stop, bus or place opens its page. Elsewhere, from the home, drops a pin and plans the way there; on a page marking a place or a pin dropped, it puts that away (Back), and a page marking nothing is left be. A trip's end is set by tapping only while its field is being typed in ("Search or tap the map").

## Map data

`.github/workflows/map.yml` downloads Geofabrik's New Hampshire extract each Monday, and `scripts/map/osm.mts` (on `@osmix/pbf`, `scripts/map/pbf.mts`) makes one generation:

- `src/map/walk.json`: the walking network around Durham;
- `src/map/gazetteer.json`: every named place, with kind, middle, extent, and buildings' outlines and doors;
- `src/map/parking.json`: OSM's parking areas, for parking's nightly check;
- vector tiles by [Planetiler](https://github.com/onthegomap/planetiler) (`scripts/map/tiles.mts`).

Its version is `<extract date>-<generator8>`, hashing the generating code, `campus.yml`, locked packages and Planetiler (`src/shared/made.mts`). It's published as the immutable release `map-data-<version>`, then `latest.json` on `map-data` moves to it (`scripts/release.mts`). A build installs its pinned generation whole, hashes checked (`installMapData` in `scripts/inputs.mts`), and fails without one; only offline builds use the fixtures. Locally (Java 21+): `node scripts/map/osm.mts <extract.osm.pbf> <planetiler.jar> [--release <folder>]`.

## Tiles and base map

Two tile sets (`src/map/basemap/tiles.mts`), at `public/data/map/tiles/<version>/<set>/{z}/{x}/{y}.pbf`, each exactly the tiles inside its boxes, with an empty file where Planetiler made none:

- **base**: OpenMapTiles, zooms 10 to 14 around Durham, Dover and Portsmouth;
- **campus**: our schema (`scripts/map/campus.yml`), zooms 14 to 16: building names, parking, doors, benches, emergency phones, water, fences, trees.

The `tiles://` protocol serves ours where a set covers, else OpenFreeMap's pinned planet build, so tiles meet without a seam. They're stored uncompressed (gzip saves a quarter, and MapLibre needs `Content-Encoding` for it); Cloudflare compresses in transit.

OpenFreeMap's Liberty style is restyled nightly (`scripts/map/style.mts`, `src/map/basemap/style.mts`) into a folder under `public/data/map/` named by its hash, which `src/map/style.json` names and which is kept one build after (`keepServedStyle`). Sprite, Latin and Greek glyphs are ours (`GLYPH_RANGES`), rarer glyphs OpenFreeMap's. Campus layers are added, hidden detail redrawn, and unnamed flowerbeds, barriers and OSM bus stops left out.

## Walking

`src/routing/graph.mts`, `walks.mts`. The network keeps each connected part of OSM's paths with twenty or more paths. A walk ends at a building's nearest door where the gazetteer knows doors. Where the network doesn't join both ends, the walk is an **estimate**, a dashed straight line, allowed 30 % over its time at 1.3 m/s against 10 % for a network walk.

## Places

- **Search** (`src/places/search.mts`, uFuzzy): gazetteer, stops, laundry and dining buildings, each name once, nearest the middle of campus, and by its aliases too: what people and the calendars call it (`ALIASES` in `src/social/aliases.mts`, which events' venues are read by too: MUB, HRC, HoCo, PCBE, T-Hall…, and `NICKNAMES`, searched by alone as they mean other things in prose: Philly, Paul, the Whitt), a hall's or library's first word ("Morse"), and a chapter's letters and nicknames. A name or alias typed exactly, case, spaces and punctuation aside, comes first, found even where the fuzzy search misses it; Greek letters are spelled out, so "ΣΧ" is "Sigma Chi". [Photon](https://photon.komoot.io) for addresses or when little is found. The home adds rooms to book ([courses.md](courses.md#rooms-to-book)), instructors, sections, rooms, parking, events, programs and the chapters the map doesn't place (`ui/Search.tsx`).
- **A tapped spot** is named by the map's label, the building it's in or a place within 60 m, the tiles' house number, else Photon (`src/places/gazetteer.mts`).
- **Unnamed or misnamed in OSM**: `src/places/added.mts` names them (HSSC, the Leavitt Lane buildings, the Dairy Bar's station, the observatory's dome…), each with the OSM way and sources it's placed by, and renames misspelled or old names (`RENAMED`: Morill, Englehardt…), each only until a generation has it right; the old names stay aliases. Jackson Estuarine Lab, at Adams Point beyond the extract's box, is added for good.
- **Fraternities and sororities** (`src/places/chapters.mts`): the chapters UNH recognizes, reviewed by hand against its chapter scorecards and Wildcat Link, as they change a few times a decade; a fetch would add a parser for one page to save a few edits. Each has its letters, council, designation, UNH's page, nicknames (each confirmed by a source, said beside it) and its house: the address and spot from Durham's parcels (the town's `Parcels_Current` layer on ArcGIS Online, whose fraternity and sorority parcels name the chapter or its house corporation) and the OSM building inside the parcel. A placed house joins the gazetteer under the chapter's name, as `kind: "building"` so taps, doors and walks treat it as one, taking the outline and doors of the building it's in in place of whatever OSM calls it, and is searched by its letters and nicknames, marked on the map (columns, white on indigo for a fraternity, plum on white for a sorority) and labelled by its letters from zoom 15.5, Greek glyphs bundled with the style; its page says its letters, chapter and council, and links to UNH's page. A chapter without a placed house is searched as its own group, saying so. To update: compare the scorecards and Wildcat Link with the list; for a new house, find its parcel on Durham's map and the building in it, and copy that building's middle as `at`.
- **Reserving and borrowing** (`src/places/services.mts`): the few ways to reserve or borrow something that belong to one place and aren't a room to book, each a line among the place's page's links: Hamel's racquetball and squash courts (`crec.unh.edu/booking`) and the library's equipment at Dimond's Info Desk. Kept by hand and short: only where it's how it's done there, never a building's list of offices.
- **Profiles** (`src/places/profiles.mts`, `ui/Profile.tsx`): a building's page says its category over its name, when it was built and last renovated among its facts, and how it stands if not in use; under Directions, its photos as a strip, opening a full-screen viewer that credits and licenses each; the verdict; its rating, "7.9 of 10 · 4th of 103", "low confidence" where the evidence was thin; and its six scores. Below what's on there, About: what it's for, its style and who designed it, and its facts, each followed by the sources it cites; its further reading joins the page's links, and the page's foot names the photographers. Each building is researched and judged by hand into one record, `src/places/profiles/<slug>.json`: name, aliases, `place` (the gazetteer's name, as the map loads it, or null for one the map has no place for), category, use, built, architect, style, status and a note on it, renovations (dated, and `major` where one renewed the building), facts citing `sources` by id, photos (Wikimedia Commons or Flickr, licensed for reuse with credit, in the order shown), `more` to read, the six `ratings` only where it's ranked (not a field, a building that's gone, not UNH's or not up yet), confidence, verdict, and `notes` and `judgeNotes` for whoever amends it. To fix a fact, add a photo or re-score a building, edit its file; `overall` and `rank` are never typed. The build makes `src/map/profiles.json` of them (`scripts/map/profiles.mts`, after the map data is installed): each record less its notes, with the sources its facts cite, the weighted mean of its scores to a tenth (exterior, interior and character a fifth each, condition and function 0.15, reputation 0.1) and its rank among every record rated, equals sharing one; it fails on a record that isn't one, a citation of a source it hasn't got, or a place the map doesn't name or two records name, and the Map data workflow runs it against a new gazetteer before publishing one. Search finds them by their other names too. Photos are served through the Worker, never Wikimedia or Flickr, which limit a campus sharing a few addresses (`GET /api/photo`, [worker.md](worker.md#api)): 500 px wide in the strip and 1280 in the viewer, each fetched as its record has it, made that wide as the best of AVIF, WebP or JPEG the browser's `Accept` names (the binding makes no JPEG XL), and kept at the edge for good, once per format. Only a photo the published profiles show is served, and a record's photo must be on Commons or Flickr, or the build fails; link Commons' 1280 px thumbnail rather than a large original. The browser's own cache keeps those seen, never the service worker; offline, a tile not seen before says what it shows. A profiled place is listed in the sitemap, and its link previews as its category, rating and verdict.
- **Laundry and dining buildings** match gazetteer buildings into `src/map/places.json` (`scripts/map/places.mts`): laundry rooms by hall name, with a small alias table, as the vendor puts every room at one spot. An unmatched one fails the build.
